action.jo — Feature Audit & Client Rebuild Plan

A structural, feature-level breakdown of the Action Mobile (Jordan) storefront and a concrete plan for building a comparable e-commerce site for your client.

Target site: action.jo Type: B2C e-commerce Market: Jordan · AR/EN Currency: JOD (د.أ.) Detected stack: Nuxt (Vue 3) SPA/SSR
Legal note before you quote a price. You can absolutely build a site with the same features and user flows. You cannot legally copy the logo, brand name (“Action / أكشن”), copywriting, product photography, category imagery, or the exact visual identity — those are the client of the original site's IP. This plan is a feature-parity rebuild for your own client's brand, not a pixel-copy.

1What the site actually is

action.jo is a mid-size consumer-electronics storefront serving Jordan. It sells phones, wearables, tablets, laptops, home appliances, cameras, smart-home devices, accessories, spare parts, and bundled offers. The catalog is organized three ways in parallel — by category, by brand, and by curated collections (new arrivals, best sellers, offers, editor's picks). It supports Arabic (RTL, default) and English, and prices are in Jordanian Dinar with promotional strike-through pricing and percent-off badges. The value proposition on the homepage centers on same-day delivery (advertised as within 6 hours), easy returns/exchange, multiple payment options, and order tracking.

4Recommended technical stack

You have three sensible paths. Pick based on time-to-launch and who will own the code after you hand it off.

A) Managed SaaS platform

Salla or Zid (both huge in the Arab market, Arabic-first, JOD-ready, built-in courier & payment integrations), or Shopify + Arabic theme. Fastest, cheapest to launch. Client pays a monthly subscription.

Timeline: 3–5 weeks. Best for: a client who wants to ship this quarter, doesn't need bespoke logic, and is fine renting the platform.

B) Open-source, self-hosted

Medusa.js (headless Node/TS + Next.js storefront) or Bagisto (Laravel + Vue) or WooCommerce (WordPress + Arabic RTL theme + WPML). Full ownership, no per-transaction fees to a platform, but you own the DevOps.

Timeline: 8–14 weeks. Best for: a client who wants to own the codebase and grow it.

C) Custom headless build (mirrors action.jo)

Nuxt 3 storefront + Laravel/Nest.js API + Postgres + Redis + S3-compatible object storage, deployed on any cloud. This is what action.jo itself is doing. Maximum flexibility, longest build.

Timeline: 14–22 weeks. Best for: a client with in-house dev capacity or a long-term product roadmap.

My recommendation for a typical Jordanian retail client: Option A (Salla or Zid) unless the client has explicitly told you they want to own the code. It gives them AR/EN, JOD, courier & payment gateway integrations, order management, and an app on day one — for a fraction of the build cost — and you can still theme it heavily. Reserve Option B/C for cases where the client has a genuine reason to reject SaaS (fee structure, custom logic, existing tech team).

7Things the client will forget to ask about

  • Product data. Someone has to type or import every SKU, spec, and image. Budget for a data-entry sprint or import from supplier feeds.
  • Photography. Consistent white-background product photos are what makes a site feel professional. Line up a photographer or a supplier feed early.
  • Content in two languages. Every product title, description, category name, and static page needs an Arabic and English version. Machine translation is a starting point, not a shipping quality.
  • Payment gateway onboarding. Jordanian acquirers (HyperPay, MEPS, Tap) require a trade license, tax number, and 1–3 weeks of paperwork. Start this on day 1.
  • Last-mile delivery. The "6-hour delivery" promise on the source site is an operational commitment, not a technical one — the site just needs to display it and route the order. The client has to actually staff or contract that.
  • Returns policy in writing. Jordan consumer-protection law requires a clear returns/warranty policy displayed on the site.
  • VAT / e-invoicing. Check current JOISC / Income & Sales Tax Department requirements for e-invoicing before launch.
  • Mobile app. Not on the source site, but almost every client asks for one 6 months in. Choose your stack (custom = React Native / Flutter; SaaS = ships one).
  • Search quality. Arabic search is hard: diacritics, plurals, English/Arabic mixing (“iPhone 17” vs “آيفون 17”). Use Meilisearch/Typesense with an Arabic tokenizer, not raw SQL LIKE.
  • RTL bugs. Every icon that “points forward” (chevrons, sliders, carousels) needs to flip. Budget time for RTL QA on every component.

8Concrete next steps

  1. Confirm with your client: SaaS (fast) or custom (owned)? This decision changes the price by ~10×.
  2. Get the client's brand book, logo, and preferred colors — you'll design a distinct identity, not a copy.
  3. Get a spreadsheet of the first 100 SKUs, with images, to seed the store and prove flows end-to-end.
  4. Kick off payment-gateway paperwork (HyperPay/MEPS/Tap) — it's the longest external dependency.
  5. Decide on courier partner(s) and same-day-delivery coverage area.
  6. Lock the site map from §2 and the priority list from §3 with your client, so scope is signed off before code starts.

✦ملخص باللغة العربية

موقع action.jo هو متجر إلكتروني أردني متوسط الحجم لبيع الأجهزة الإلكترونية (موبايلات، ساعات، تابلت، لابتوب، أجهزة منزلية، كاميرات، اكسسوارات، قطع صيانة، بندلز). المتجر مبني على Nuxt 3 واجهته أمامية، ويدعم اللغتين العربية (الافتراضية RTL) والإنجليزية (/en)، والعملة الدينار الأردني.

الميزات الأساسية التي يجب توفيرها لعميلك:

  • كتالوج بفئات متعددة المستويات + صفحات لكل علامة تجارية (~50) + صفحات منتج مفصلة (معرض صور، خيارات، مخزون، سعر مع خصم).
  • بحث مع اقتراحات فورية، فلاتر (السعر، العلامة التجارية، التوفر)، ترتيب النتائج.
  • عربة تسوق (Drawer + صفحة)، كوبونات خصم، دفع عند الاستلام + بطاقة (HyperPay/Tap/MEPS)، ومحفظة CliQ، وتقسيط Tabby/Tamara.
  • تسجيل دخول / إنشاء حساب مع OTP عبر SMS (ضروري في السوق الأردني)، الملف الشخصي، دفتر عناوين، سجل الطلبات، تتبع الطلب.
  • لوحة تحكم إدارية كاملة: منتجات، فئات، طلبات، عملاء، كوبونات، بانرات، تقارير، أدوار (مدير/محرر/مندوب).
  • صفحات ثابتة (من نحن، الأسئلة الشائعة، طرق الشراء، اتصل بنا، الشكاوى، سياسة الخصوصية) قابلة للتحرير من CMS.
  • تحليلات: Google Tag Manager + Meta Pixel + Microsoft Clarity + GA4.
  • SEO: sitemap، JSON-LD، hreflang عربي/إنجليزي، Open Graph.

توصيتي: إذا كان العميل يريد الإطلاق بسرعة وبأقل تكلفة، استخدم سلة (Salla) أو زد (Zid) — منصات جاهزة تدعم العربية والدينار الأردني وشركات الشحن والدفع محلياً. إذا كان يريد امتلاك الكود بالكامل، ابنِ بـ Nuxt 3 + Laravel على غرار المتجر الأصلي.

ملاحظة قانونية مهمة: يمكنك بناء موقع بنفس الميزات وتجربة المستخدم، لكن لا يمكنك نسخ الاسم التجاري (Action / أكشن)، الشعار، النصوص، صور المنتجات، أو الهوية البصرية — كل هذه ملكية فكرية للمتجر الأصلي. يجب أن تصمم هوية جديدة لعميلك.
